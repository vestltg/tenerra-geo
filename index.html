We're building personal knowledge infrastructure so that the knowledge we build with AI remains ours, travels with us, and continues to serve us throughout life.

AI gives us access to the knowledge of the world at our fingertips. But knowledge alone has no purpose. It becomes useful only when we apply it to our own lives, in our own circumstances, at the moment we need it.

Personal knowledge is not a fact we capture once and file away. It is shaped by three things that are in constant transition: time, the evolving conditions of our lives, and the moment in which knowledge is used.

As life unfolds, knowledge captured perfectly yesterday may no longer be useful today.

That is why no single AI chat, however thorough, can provide continuity of knowing. Useful personal knowledge must be maintained as life happens — continuously, in context, and for as long as it matters.

Today, the personal knowledge we build with AI about our own lives remains isolated in LLM silos. An AI platform may retain fragments of our history, but it does not carry forward the organized, usable context of a life. Close the tab, change the platform, or enter a new chapter of life, and the context that made the knowledge useful is left behind.

Tenerra is building systems that hold our personal knowledge in context for life — not at the edge of a platform.

ANYA is our first product: Personal Knowledge Continuity for parents of children with intellectual and developmental disabilities. It gives parents a place to preserve what they know about their child — in their own words, under their control, for the people who may need it, for as long as it is needed. Explore ANYA at anya.tenerra.ai.
